# Privacy Policy

**Substitution** is available on Android, iOS, Web, Windows, Linux and macOS.  
Contact: hello@substitution.art

---

## 1. Matrix Protocol

Substitution uses the Matrix protocol. Substitution is a **client only** — it does not host or control any servers. All data (posts, messages, files) is stored on the Matrix homeserver you choose to connect to. The data protection policy of that homeserver applies to you.

For convenience, one or more public servers are offered as defaults. The Substitution developers do not guarantee their trustworthiness. Users are informed of which server they are connecting to before they do so.

More information: [https://matrix.org](https://matrix.org)

## 2. Local Database Cache

Substitution caches some data received from the server in a local SQLite database on your device. This cache is cleared when you log out. You can also delete all locally stored data by deleting the app.

## 3. Encryption

All communication between Substitution and your chosen Matrix homeserver is protected by **transport encryption** (TLS). Substitution additionally supports **end-to-end encryption** (E2EE) for messages, so that only conversation participants can read them.

## 4. App Permissions

### Internet Access
Required to communicate with the Matrix server.

### Read External Storage / Files
Users may share files and images from the device. Files are only uploaded when explicitly chosen by the user.

### Push Notifications
Substitution **will** use push notifications to alert you of new direct messages and trending posts in your followed feeds. When push notifications are enabled:

- A push notification token is generated by Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM) and forwarded to your Matrix homeserver's push gateway.
- The notification payload sent through the gateway contains only the minimum required data (e.g. room ID, event ID) — the actual message content is fetched from the homeserver on your device.
- You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's system settings.
- The Substitution developers do not operate a push gateway; this is handled by your homeserver operator.

## 5. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (**Settings → Delete Account**). When you delete your account:

- A request is sent to your homeserver to deactivate the account and erase all associated content (`erase: true`).
- The local database cache is cleared immediately.
- Actual data retention depends on the policy of your chosen homeserver.

This implements your right to erasure under GDPR Art. 17.

## 6. Data We Do Not Collect

Substitution itself (as a client application distributed by Florian Minnecker) does **not** collect, store, or process any personal data on its own servers. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, and no advertising network integrated into the app.

## 7. Age Restrictions — Not for Children

Substitution is a social networking application intended for users **aged 16 and over**.

- **Australia** (Online Safety Amendment Act 2024): Substitution is not directed at, and is not intended to be used by, persons under **16 years of age**.
- **United Kingdom** (Children's Code / AADC): We apply the principle of high privacy by default. The service is intended for users 16 and over.
- **European Union / GDPR**: The minimum age for digital consent is 16 (or the applicable minimum in your country). Users under this age must not use the service without verifiable parental consent.
- **United States** (COPPA): Substitution is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
- **Germany** (JMStV): The service is not intended for users under 16.

If you are aware of a user under the applicable minimum age, please report this to **hello@substitution.art**. We comply with applicable age-related regulations by maintaining this policy and setting appropriate age ratings in app stores (17+ on Apple App Store, Mature 17+ on Google Play).
